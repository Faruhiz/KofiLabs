Connect Cloudflare
Your Worker will be deployed to your Cloudflare account — free tier is more than enough.
Get your API Token:
  1. Go to dash.cloudflare.com/profile/api-tokens
  2. Click Create Token → use the Edit Cloudflare Workers template
  3. In Account Resources, select your account
  4. In Zone Resources, select "All zones"
  5. Click Continue to Summary → Create Token
  6. Copy the token and paste it below
Needs Workers Scripts:Edit permission. Never sent to any server — used only from your browser.
Connected! Your Cloudflare account is ready.
Connect Streamlabs
Click below to authorise via Streamlabs. A browser tab will open — log in and click Approve. You'll be brought straight back.
Ko-fi verification token
Ko-fi signs every webhook with a token so your Worker can verify it's genuine.
Find your token:
  1. Go to ko-fi.com/manage/webhooks
  2. Extend the Advanced tab and copy the Verification Token shown on that page
  3. You'll paste your Worker URL back here after deploy
This gets stored as an encrypted Worker secret. Ko-fi will reject webhooks if they don't match.
The name your Worker appears as in Cloudflare dashboard.
